## 2. Afternoon & Practical Laboratory Slots
- **L1 to L12**: Practical / Laboratory 2-hour or 3-hour blocks (e.g., L4: Thursday 14:00–17:00).
- **M7, M10**: Practical lab sessions (0-0-4).
- **NG1 to NG3**: Non-Graded course modules (conducted in designated evening or weekend slots).
- **CA, CB, CC**: Common institute core lecture slots.
- **T**: Thesis / Project hours (individually scheduled with supervisor).
