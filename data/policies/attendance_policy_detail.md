# Attendance Policy — IIT Jodhpur

> **Effective:** Semester I, Academic Year 2024-25 onwards (per Amendment Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51682 dated 08 July 2024)

---

## 1. Minimum Attendance Requirement

- Every student must maintain a **minimum of 75% attendance** in each course individually.
- Attendance is calculated **per course** — satisfying the requirement in one course does not compensate for deficiency in another.
- Both lectures and tutorials/labs count separately toward attendance for that component.

---

## 2. Attendance Categories and Consequences

| Attendance Range | Status | Consequence |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| ≥ 75% | Satisfactory | Eligible to appear in Major examination |
| 65% – 74% | Conditional | May apply for **condonation** to course instructor with valid medical/official reason |
| < 65% | Debarred | **DX grade** awarded; barred from Major examination |

> [!WARNING]
> A **DX grade** is treated as equivalent to **F** for CGPA purposes. Avoid attendance below 65% under all circumstances.

---

## 3. DX Grade (Debarred)

- **DX** stands for *Debarred from Examination*.
- Awarded when attendance falls **below 65%** (amended from the earlier threshold of 75%).
- Students awarded DX in a course:
  - **Cannot** appear in the Major (End-Semester) Examination.
  - Receive DX on their transcript.
  - Must repeat the course in the next offering.
- DX in a **Programme Core (PC)** course requires mandatory repeat.

---

## 4. Medical Leaves and Relaxation

### 4.1 IMO-Certified Medical Leave
- Medical leaves certified by the **Institute Medical Officer (IMO)** are considered separately.
- Such periods may **not** be counted toward absenteeism, subject to:
  - Submission of valid medical certificate from IMO within **3 working days** of recovery.
  - Approval by the **course instructor** and **Faculty Advisor**.
- Students must collect a Medical Leave Certificate from the Health Centre.

### 4.2 Condonation (65%–74% Attendance)
- Students with attendance between **65% and 74%** may apply for condonation.
- Procedure:
  1. Submit a written application to the **course instructor** stating reasons.
  2. Attach supporting documents (medical certificate, event proof, etc.).
  3. Course instructor forwards recommendation to the **Dean (Academics)** for final approval.
- Condonation is **not automatic** — it is at the discretion of the instructor and Dean.

### 4.3 Official Institute Representation
- Students representing IIT Jodhpur in official competitions, sports, cultural events (with prior sanction) may have those days excluded from absenteeism.
- Prior sanction from the **Dean (Student Affairs)** is mandatory.

---

## 5. Attendance Tracking and Reporting

- Faculty must upload attendance records on the **ERP portal within 48 hours** of each class.
- Students can view their real-time attendance on the Student ERP Portal (`erp.iitj.ac.in`).
- The **Faculty Advisor** is notified automatically when a student's attendance falls below 80% in any course (early warning).
- Attendance notices are issued to students at **80%** and again at **75%** thresholds.

---

## 6. Special Cases

### 6.1 Newly Admitted Students (Late Joiners)
- Students who join late due to admission processes will have their attendance calculated from the date of joining, subject to Dean approval.

### 6.2 Semester Withdrawal
- A student who withdraws from the semester officially will not be awarded DX; the semester is not counted.

### 6.3 Medical Semester Withdrawal
- In case of prolonged illness (4+ consecutive weeks), a student may apply for **Medical Semester Withdrawal** to the Dean (Academics) with full medical documentation.

---

## 7. Procedure for Attendance Disputes

1. Check your attendance on the ERP portal.
2. If discrepancy found, contact the course instructor within **7 days** of the class.
3. If not resolved, submit a written complaint to the **Department Academic Committee (DAC)**.
4. DAC decision is final unless escalated to Dean (Academics) within 5 working days.
