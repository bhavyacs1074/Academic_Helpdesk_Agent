# Leave Rules — IIT Jodhpur

> **Reference:** Academic Regulations, IIT Jodhpur — Sections 15 (UG) and 23 (PG)

---

## Part A: Undergraduate (UG) Leave Rules (Section 15)

### 1. Types of Leave for UG Students

#### 1.1 Short Leave (Casual Leave)
- Duration: Up to **3 consecutive working days** within a semester.
- Purpose: Personal, family, or short-duration medical reasons.
- Procedure:
  - Inform the **Faculty Advisor** in advance (or as soon as possible).
  - Submit leave application on the Student ERP Portal.
  - No formal approval needed for up to 3 days, but Faculty Advisor must be notified.
- **Note:** Short leave days are counted toward absenteeism for attendance purposes unless covered by a valid medical certificate.

#### 1.2 Medical Leave
- Duration: As prescribed by the physician; no fixed upper limit within a semester.
- Conditions:
  - Certificate from **Institute Medical Officer (IMO)** or a registered medical practitioner.
  - IMO-certified medical leave may be excluded from attendance computation (see Attendance Policy).
  - Submit medical certificate within **3 working days** of return.
- Procedure:
  1. Obtain medical certificate from IMO / hospital.
  2. Submit to **Faculty Advisor** and upload on ERP Portal.
  3. Faculty Advisor informs course instructors.

#### 1.3 Semester Withdrawal (SW)
- A student may withdraw from an **entire semester** for valid reasons (medical, family emergency, personal hardship).
- Conditions:
  - Application must be submitted to **Dean (Academics)** before the end of the first **4 weeks** of the semester.
  - Withdrawal after 4 weeks requires additional justification and DRC approval.
  - Withdrawal does not count toward the maximum duration limit for the degree.
- Consequence:
  - No grades are awarded for the withdrawn semester.
  - The semester appears as **SW** on the transcript.
  - Student must re-register for the same semester in the next academic year.

#### 1.4 Academic Leave (Internship / Exchange)
- For students participating in approved academic internships, student exchange programs, or research fellowships.
- Must be sanctioned by **Dean (Academics)** in advance.
- Credits may be transferred subject to DRC approval.

---

### 2. Cancellation of Semester (UG)

- If a student is absent for **more than 4 continuous weeks** without prior sanction:
  - The semester may be **cancelled** by the Academic Office.
  - The student must apply for re-admission for the semester.
  - This counts toward the maximum duration of the degree program.

## Part B: Postgraduate (PG) Leave Rules (Section 23)

### 1. MTech Students

| Leave Type | Maximum Duration | Conditions |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Annual Leave | **30 days per year** | Includes all casual, medical, and vacation leave |
| Single stretch | **≤ 10 consecutive days** | Beyond 10 days requires DRC Convener approval |
| Medical Leave | As required | IMO certificate mandatory |

- MTech students must seek leave approval from the **DRC (Departmental Research Committee) Convener**.
- Leave during project/thesis periods requires additional approval from the **Thesis Supervisor**.

### 2. PhD Students

| Leave Type | Maximum Duration | Conditions |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Annual Leave | **30 days per year** | Includes all casual and medical leave |
| Single stretch | **≤ 15 consecutive days** | Beyond 15 days requires DRC Convener + Dean (Academics) approval |
| Maternity/Paternity Leave | As per Govt. of India norms | |
| Academic Leave (conference, field work) | Sanctioned separately | Supervisor + DRC approval required |

### 3. Leave Application Procedure (PG)

1. Submit leave application to **Thesis/Project Supervisor** (for thesis period) or **Faculty Advisor** (for coursework period).
2. Supervisor forwards to **DRC Convener** for approval.
3. For extended leave (>10 days for MTech, >15 days for PhD), DRC Convener submits to **Dean (Academics)** for final sanction.
4. Approved leave is recorded in the ERP system.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> PG students (PhD/MTech) must be physically present for the **minimum residence requirement**. Excessive leaves may be counted toward the minimum residence period and could delay degree completion.

## Part C: General Rules (All Students)

### Medical Certificate Requirements
- Must be from a **registered medical practitioner** (MBBS or higher) or IMO.
- Must mention the diagnosis (or nature of illness without personal details if privacy is a concern), dates of illness, and fitness-to-return date.
- Self-declarations are **not accepted** as medical certificates.
- Certificates must be submitted within **3 working days** of return from leave.

### Attendance Impact of Leave
- Approved medical leaves (IMO-certified) may be excluded from attendance computation.
- Other types of leave (casual, personal) are counted toward absence for attendance.
- Refer to the [Attendance Policy](attendance_policy_detail.md) for details on attendance thresholds.

### Leave During Examinations
- Students who need to miss an examination due to leave must follow the [Makeup Examination Policy](makeup_exam_policy.md).
- Leave approval does **not** automatically entitle a student to a makeup exam; a separate application is required.
