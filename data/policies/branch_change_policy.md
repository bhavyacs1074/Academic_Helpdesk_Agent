# Branch Change Policy — IIT Jodhpur

## 1. Eligibility Criteria for Change of Branch
Undergraduate students (B.Tech. / B.S.) are eligible to apply for a change of branch/discipline at the end of their first year (after completion of Semester II).

### Core Eligibility Conditions:
1. **E1**: Must have completed all prescribed credits for Semesters I and II (including all non-graded components).
2. **E2**: Must have **no backlogs** (no active 'F' or 'E' grades).
3. **E3**: Must have **no pending disciplinary actions** or penalties.

## 2. Minimum CGPA Requirements

| After Semester | Target Branch | Minimum CGPA (GEN / OBC / EWS) | Minimum CGPA (SC / ST / PwD) | Sanctioned Strength Limits |
|----------------|---------------|--------------------------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Sem II | Any Engineering Branch | **CGPA ≥ 8.5** | **CGPA ≥ 7.5** | Branch strength must not fall below 90% or exceed 110% |
| Sem II | Engineering Science (ES) | **CGPA ≥ 8.0** | **CGPA ≥ 7.0** | Lower limit 90% of strength; max 30 seats |

## 3. Important Rules
- Branch change is considered strictly on merit based on CGPA earned at the end of Semester II.
- No branch change is permitted after the second year onwards.
- **Senate Notice**: Phase-out / discontinuation of traditional branch change begins from AY 2026–27 per institute policy updates.
