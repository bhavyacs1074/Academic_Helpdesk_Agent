# Makeup Examination Policy — IIT Jodhpur

> **Reference:** Academic Regulations Section 8 | Amendment Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/52683 dated 08 July 2024

---

## 1. Overview

IIT Jodhpur provides for **makeup (supplementary) examinations** for students who are unable to appear in scheduled examinations due to valid, documented reasons. Makeup exams are available for:

- **Minor I Examination** (approximately Week 7–8 of semester)
- **Minor II Examination** (approximately Week 13–14 of semester)
- **Major (End-Semester) Examination**

> [!NOTE]
> No student is permitted more than **one makeup exam per course per semester**.

---

## 2. Eligibility Conditions

A student is eligible to apply for a makeup examination only under the following conditions:

| Reason | Documentation Required |
|:---|:---|
| Medical emergency (hospitalization or serious illness) | Certificate from Institute Medical Officer (IMO) or recognized hospital |
| Death in immediate family (parents, siblings, spouse, children) | Death certificate + leave sanction from Faculty Advisor |
| Official institute representation (sports, cultural, academic) | Prior sanction letter from Dean (Student Affairs) / Competent authority |
| Natural calamity / Civil disturbance affecting travel | Documentary proof accepted by Dean (Academics) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The reason must be beyond the student's control. Pre-planned events, personal travel, or lack of preparation are **not** valid grounds.

---

## 3. Application Procedure

### 3.1 For Minor Examination Makeup

1. Submit a written application to the **course instructor** within **3 working days** of the original examination.
2. Attach all supporting documents (medical certificate, official letter, etc.).
3. Instructor reviews and approves or rejects the application.
4. If approved, the instructor schedules the makeup exam within **2 weeks** of the original exam date.

### 3.2 For Major (End-Semester) Examination Makeup

1. Submit a written application to the **Office of Academics (OAA)** within **3 working days** of the original examination.
2. Attach all supporting documents.
3. Application is reviewed by the **Dean (Academics)**.
4. If approved by Dean, the Office of Academics schedules the makeup Major exam.
5. The makeup Major exam is typically held **within 2 weeks** after the regular Major exam schedule ends.

---

## 4. Approval Authority

| Examination | Approval Authority |
|:---|:---|
| Minor I Makeup | Course Instructor |
| Minor II Makeup | Course Instructor |
| Major Exam Makeup | Dean (Academics) |

---

## 5. Evaluation Weightage

| Component | Weightage |
|:---|:---:|
| Minor I | 20% |
| Minor II | 20% |
| Major (End-Semester) | 40% |
| Assignments / Quizzes / Lab / Project | 20% |

> [!NOTE]
> The exact weightage may vary per course as communicated by the instructor at the start of the semester. Refer to the **Course Information Sheet** distributed in the first week.

---

## 6. Restrictions

- A student who appeared in the original exam **cannot** apply for a makeup exam for the same exam.
- A student with a **DX grade** (attendance deficiency) is **not eligible** for a makeup Major exam.
- Makeup exams are **not available** for quiz components, lab evaluations, or continuous assessment items within the 20% internal component.
- No makeup exam is held for the makeup exam itself — if a student misses the makeup, they receive a zero for that component.

---

## 7. Exam Conduct Rules

- Makeup exams follow the same rules as regular examinations (no electronic devices, ID required, etc.).
- The question paper for makeup exams may differ from the original but covers the same syllabus.
- Grading follows the same rubric.
