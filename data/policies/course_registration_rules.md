# Course Registration Rules & Regulations — IIT Jodhpur

## 1. General Registration Rules
Every student must register for prescribed courses and credits as per their programme curriculum on the dates specified in the Academic Calendar approved by the Senate.

- **Regular Registration**: Must be completed by the specified registration date before the semester begins.
- **Provisional Registration**: Allowed for new students awaiting final qualifying degree results, subject to passing proof submission by the calendar deadline.
- **Late Registration**: Permitted with a late fee penalty within the window announced in the Academic Calendar. Students failing to register by the late registration date will be deregistered. Reinstatement requires Senate Chairman approval through Dean (Academics) with a fine.

## 2. Adding and Dropping of Courses
- A student may **add or drop** course(s) with the approval of their Faculty Advisor until the last date of registration specified in the Academic Calendar.
- Dropped courses do not appear on the student's Grade Card or transcript.

## 3. Course Withdrawal
- After the add/drop deadline, a student may withdraw from a course within **four weeks** from the beginning of the semester.
- A withdrawn course is awarded a **'W' grade** on the Grade Card/transcript.
- 'W' grade does not affect the calculation of SGPA or CGPA.

## 4. Semester Cancellation
- Absence from classes for **four (4) or more weeks** during a semester results in automatic cancellation of semester registration from all courses.

## 5. Credit Limits
### Undergraduate (B.Tech.)
- **Minimum credits per regular semester**: 12 credits (probation under-load allows 16 max).
- **Maximum credits per regular semester**: 25 credits.
- **Overloading**: Permitted only for students with CGPA ≥ 7.5 or those requiring ≤ 70 credits to graduate.
- **Independent/Self-Study**: Maximum 4 credits per semester.

### Postgraduate (M.Tech. & M.Sc.)
- **M.Sc.**: Minimum 10 credits, Maximum 24 credits per semester.
- **M.Tech. (Full-Time Regular)**: Minimum 10 credits, Maximum 17 credits per regular semester.
- **Ph.D. (Regular)**: Minimum 6 credits, Maximum 12 credits per regular semester.

## 6. Summer Term Registration
- Summer registration is for academically deficient students, backlog clearing, or students within 8 credits of graduating.
- Maximum credit registration in summer: **8 credits**.
- A summer course runs only if department faculty is available and approved by Dean (Academics).
