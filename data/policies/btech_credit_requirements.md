# B.Tech. Degree Credit & Curriculum Requirements — IIT Jodhpur

## 1. Overview of B.Tech. Credit Framework
To earn a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) degree at IIT Jodhpur, an undergraduate student must satisfy all credit and non-graded curricular requirements specified in the Academic Regulations.

## 3. Open Elective (OE) Requirements
- **Required Open Elective Credits**: A student in the regular B.Tech. program must earn at least **10 to 12 Open Elective (OE) credits** to qualify for graduation.
- Open electives may be chosen from any department outside the student's parent discipline, including School of Liberal Arts (Humanities), Management, Design, or interdisciplinary platforms.
- Independent study courses (up to 3 credits) can also be counted toward open elective requirements.

## 4. Minimum Duration and Residency
- Minimum residency for regular B.Tech.: **6 semesters**.
- Maximum permissible duration to complete B.Tech.: **14 registered semesters**.
- Co-curricular requirements: At least 2 semesters of engagement in NCC, NSS, NSO, or performing arts.
