# Grading System — IIT Jodhpur

> This document describes the grading system used at IIT Jodhpur as per the Academic Regulations (approved July 2019, amended 2024).

---

## 1. Letter Grades and Grade Points

| Letter Grade | Description | Grade Points |
|:---:|:---|:---:|
| **O** | Outstanding | 10 |
| **A+** | Excellent | 9 |
| **A** | Very Good | 8 |
| **B+** | Good | 7 |
| **B** | Above Average | 6 |
| **C** | Average | 5 |
| **D** | Marginal Pass | 4 |
| **F** | Fail | 0 |
| **I** | Incomplete | — |
| **W** | Withdrawn | — |
| **DX** | Debarred (attendance) | — |
| **NG** | Non-Graded (Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory) | — |

> [!NOTE]
> Grades **I**, **W**, **DX**, and **NG** are not included in SGPA/CGPA calculations. An **I** grade must be resolved within the next semester, failing which it converts to **F**.

## 2. Grade Point Average Calculations

### 2.1 Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)

$$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum_{i} (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum_{i} C_i}$$

Where:
- $C_i$ = Credits of course $i$ in the semester
- $G_i$ = Grade points earned in course $i$
- Summation is over all graded courses in the semester (F grade is included)

**Example:**

| Course | Credits | Grade | Grade Points | Weighted |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| CSL2020 | 4 | A | 8 | 32 |
| MAL1020 | 4 | B+ | 7 | 28 |
| PHL1010 | 3 | O | 10 | 30 |
| EEL1010 | 4 | A+ | 9 | 36 |
| **Total** | **15** | | | **126** |

$$\text{SGPA} = \frac{126}{15} = 8.40$$

### 2.2 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum_{j} (C_j \times G_j)}{\sum_{j} C_j}$$

Where:
- Summation is over **all semesters** completed
- $C_j$ and $G_j$ denote credits and grade points for all graded courses across semesters

> [!TIP]
> CGPA is computed at the end of each semester and appears on the grade sheet. Only courses with grades O, A+, A, B+, B, C, D, and F are counted toward CGPA. Repeat courses (after F) replace the old grade.

## 3. Special Grade Conditions

### 3.1 Incomplete (I) Grade
- Awarded when a student cannot complete coursework due to genuine emergency.
- Must be resolved (converted to a regular letter grade) before the end of the **subsequent semester**.
- If not resolved, the **I** grade automatically converts to **F**.

### 3.2 Withdrawn (W) Grade
- Awarded when a student officially withdraws from a course before the **last date of course withdrawal** (as per the academic calendar).
- **W** does not affect CGPA.
- Appears on the transcript but is not counted in credit/grade calculations.

### 3.3 DX Grade (Debarred)
- Awarded to students who fail to meet the minimum **75% attendance** requirement.
- Students with a DX grade are **debarred from appearing in the Major (End-Semester) Examination**.
- DX is treated equivalent to **F** for CGPA purposes.

### 3.4 NG (Non-Graded) Courses
- Courses evaluated on a **Satisfactory / Unsatisfactory (S/U)** basis.
- Examples: Professional Ethics, NSS/NCC, Physical Education.
- Not counted in CGPA.

---

## 4. Minimum Academic Requirements

| Requirement | Threshold |
|:---|:---:|
| Minimum CGPA to continue (UG) | 5.00 |
| Minimum CGPA for Honours | 8.00 |
| Minimum CGPA for PhD direct admission | 7.00 |
| Grade for passing a course | D (4 grade points) or above |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A student who earns an **F** grade in a **Programme Core (PC)** course must repeat the course. An **F** in an elective may be replaced by another elective subject to DRC approval.

---

## 5. Conversion to Percentage (Indicative)

IIT Jodhpur does not officially convert CGPA to percentage. However, for external purposes:

$$\text{Percentage (indicative)} = \text{CGPA} \times 10$$

This formula is printed on official transcripts issued by the Office of Academics.

---

## 6. Grade Sheet and Transcript

- **Grade Sheet**: Issued at the end of each semester, shows SGPA and running CGPA.
- **Consolidated Transcript**: Issued on request; shows all courses, grades, SGPA per semester, and final CGPA.
- **Degree Certificate**: Issued at convocation, mentions final CGPA.
