# Academic Helpdesk FAQ & Knowledge Base

### Q1: What is the attendance requirement to sit in the Major exam?
**Answer**: A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course. If attendance falls between 65% and 75% due to valid medical reasons, condonation may be considered by the course instructor/IMO. If attendance is below 65%, the student is awarded a DX grade and debarred from the Major exam.
*Source: Academic Regulations Section 4.1.9, Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51682.*

### Q4: How many Open Elective credits do I need to earn in order to get a B.Tech. Degree?
**Answer**: To earn a regular B.Tech. degree at IIT Jodhpur, an undergraduate student must earn at least **10 to 12 Open Elective (OE) credits** (Table 11.3, Section 11.3).
*Source: Academic Regulations, Section 11.3 (Table 11.3).*

### Q6: What should I do if I missed Minor 1 due to illness?
**Answer**: Submit a formal application for a make-up examination along with a medical certificate verified by the Institute Medical Officer (IMO) to the course instructor within 3 working days of the missed exam.
*Source: Academic Regulations Section 8, Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/52683.*

### Q7: Can I drop a course after the semester has begun?
**Answer**: Yes, you can add or drop a course until the Add/Drop deadline (usually 10-12 days into the semester) with the approval of your Faculty Advisor. Afterward, you may formally withdraw from a course with a 'W' grade until the 4th week of classes.
*Source: Academic Regulations Section 3.3 and 3.4.*

### Q8: What is the maximum duration to complete a B.Tech. degree?
**Answer**: The maximum duration permitted to complete the B.Tech. program is **14 registered semesters**.
*Source: Academic Regulations Section 11.4.*

### Q9: What happens if a question is out of academic scope (e.g. dining options or city transport)?
**Answer**: The Academic Helpdesk Agent politely states that it does not possess that information, does not hallucinate, and logs the question in the human review queue.
