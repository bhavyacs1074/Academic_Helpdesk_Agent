# Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/51682
## Subject: Amendment in Attendance Policy of the Institute
**Date of Notification**: 08 July 2024  
**Issuing Authority**: Office of Academic Affairs (OAA), Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur  
**Target Audience**: All Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Ph.D. Students; All Faculty Instructors

### Official Order:
Pursuant to the resolution of the Senate in its meeting, the following attendance guidelines are effective starting Semester I of Academic Year 2024-25:

1. **Mandatory 75% Requirement**: Full regular attendance is expected in every course. A minimum of **75% attendance** across lectures, tutorials, and practicals is compulsory to be eligible to appear in the Major Examination.
2. **Medical Condonation (65% to 75%)**: Students falling between 65% and 75% attendance due to documented medical grounds must have their medical certificates validated by the Institute Medical Officer (IMO) within 5 working days of recovery.
3. **DX Grade (Debarred)**: Students with attendance strictly **below 65%** (or below 75% without valid condonation) will be awarded a **DX (Debarred)** grade and will **NOT be permitted to sit for the Major Examination**.
4. **Grading Penalty for Marginal Attendance**: Even if permitted to sit for the exam under special condonation with attendance below 75%, students must score at least a 'C' grade; otherwise an 'F' grade will be recorded.
5. **Instructor Logging**: All course instructors are required to record and post attendance updates on the ERP portal on a weekly basis.
