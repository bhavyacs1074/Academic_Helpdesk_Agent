# Office Order: Branch Change Guidelines for B.Tech. Batch
**Office of Academic Affairs, IIT Jodhpur**  
**Order Ref**: IITJ/OAA/BC/2024-25/01

Applications are invited from eligible B.Tech. 1st year students after completion of Semester II for discipline change:

1. **Eligibility**:
   - Completed all 1st year credits with zero backlogs (no F/E/I grades).
   - Minimum CGPA at end of Sem II:
     - General / OBC / EWS: **CGPA ≥ 8.5**
     - SC / ST / PwD: **CGPA ≥ 7.5**
2. **Branch Strength Restrictions**:
   - Sanctioned intake cannot decrease below 90% or increase above 110%.
3. **Application Window**:
   - Portal opens 3 days after Sem II result declaration and remains active for 5 working days.
