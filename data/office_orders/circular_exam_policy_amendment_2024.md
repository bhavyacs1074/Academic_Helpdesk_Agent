# Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/52683
## Subject: Amendment in Examination and Evaluation Policy
**Date of Notification**: 08 July 2024  
**Issuing Authority**: Office of Academic Affairs (OAA), IIT Jodhpur  
**Target Audience**: All Students and Teaching Faculty

### Examination Components and Guidelines:
1. **Mandatory Examination Components**: For all standard 14-week courses, continuous evaluation shall comprise:
   - **Minor 1 Examination**: 60 minutes duration (typically Weeks 5-6)
   - **Minor 2 Examination**: 60 minutes duration (typically Weeks 10-11)
   - **Major Examination**: 120 minutes duration (End-semester centrally scheduled)
2. **Make-up Examination Policy**:
   - Make-up examinations are **not granted as a matter of right**.
   - Permissible solely on genuine medical grounds (hospitalization verified by IMO) or dire emergency/institute representation.
   - The student **must submit a formal make-up request within 3 working days** (maximum 10 days for prolonged hospitalization) to the Dean (Academics) through the course instructor.
   - Make-up for **Major Exam** requires formal sanction from Dean (Academics).
   - Make-up for **Minor Exam** requires written approval from the respective Course Instructor.
   - Only **one make-up attempt** is permitted per course per semester.
3. **No Prorating**: Missing an exam without an approved make-up will result in zero marks. Prorating marks from other exams is strictly disallowed.
